Source analysis only; no device access. At 2160², each eye contains 4.67 million pixels. Coverage depends on head pose and scene. The handoff's in-pass timestamps are tile-coarse proportions, not absolute eye costs; all proposed gains below are speculative and non-additive.

## Coverage and fragment work

- **Background spheres and ground:** widest coverage. Native BG2 spheres can cover essentially the whole eye; photographic landscapes cover the upper hemisphere (roughly half a level-looking view), or a full sphere for space scenes. BG3, orbital surfaces and celestial patches add scene-dependent overlap (`src/vr/background_tiles.cpp:94`, `:161`, `:329`, `:636`; `src/vr/application.cpp:1377`, `:1412`, `:1439`). Stars/backgrounds/tunnel draw before models, sprites and cabin, normally without depth testing. Hidden opaque world/cabin pixels still incur background shading. Visibility masking rejects lens-hidden pixels, but its ordered layers use depth zero and therefore do not reject ordinary geometry (`src/vr/vulkan_scene_pipeline.cpp:38`, `:161`).
- **Native tile shader:** no hardware texture sampling; reads `StructuredBuffer<uint>`. Each surviving pixel decodes two tile-map bytes, 2/4/8 bitplane bytes for 2/4/8-bpp art, then a palette word, plus control/header reads. Integer wrapping, flips, mosaics, priority and atlas-region branches precede transparency rejection; optional scanline/column offsets add reads. Off-screen vertical-offset extrapolation may scan 32 columns (64 byte-read calls), unless the vertex-computed horizon fast path applies (`src/vr/shaders/scene.hlsl:556`, `:605`, `:704`). Orbital continuation adds normalize/atan2 and seven 3D noise evaluations (eight corner hashes each), derivatives and palette thresholds (`:775`, `:760`, `:803`).
- **Enhanced backdrop shader:** manual trilinear filtering reads eight packed RGBA pixels, plus mip metadata, modulo/clamp, derivatives, log2 and lerps; unpremultiplies, applies palette/brightness, and optionally performs three-channel sRGB pow conversion. Orbital surfaces evaluate two filtered projections: sixteen RGBA reads plus normalize/atan2 and blends (`scene.hlsl:842`, `:866`, `:874`). Full-eye eight-sample coverage implies 37.3 million RGBA reads / 149 MB logical data per eye before cache reuse; this is not measured memory traffic. Pole replacement can add a read. No fragment loop here.
- **Surrounding stars:** small billboard pairs, not a full-screen star shader. Transform, recycling, radial distance and palette reads happen per vertex; fragments largely return vertex colour with visibility/style branches, no per-pixel texel reads (`src/vr/source_models.cpp:17`, `:437`; `scene.hlsl:452`, `:1074`). Tunnel surround is broad flat-colour geometry (`background_tiles.cpp:132`).
- **Cabin/HUD:** cabin frame/bulkhead may fill large peripheral areas or almost the eye when looking away. Front/rear colours and sRGB conversion are prepared on CPU: zero fragment fetches for those flat faces. Ship hull retains native materials. HUD covers a compact instrument band; OAM pixels decode four bitplane bytes and a palette word, discard transparent ink; meters are flat rectangles. Cabin/HUD depth-test, but come after the sky (`src/vr/cockpit.cpp:15`, `:110`, `:219`; `src/vr/source_sprites.cpp:50`, `:357`; `scene.hlsl:822`; `application.cpp:1767`).

## Ranked proposals

1. **Hardware-filtered backdrop images/samplers.** Replace storage-buffer manual filtering with sampled UNORM mip images; preserve current encoded-space premultiplied filtering, pole treatment and subsequent colour response. Estimate 20–50% less backdrop shader time; medium risk (seams, alpha, mip equivalence). Shader/descriptors/upload changes: `scene.hlsl:842`; `include/starfox/vr/backdrop_texture.hpp:36`; `src/vr/vulkan_draw_packets.cpp:387`.
2. **Diagnostic single-mip bilinear backdrop mode.** Four rather than eight RGBA reads (eight rather than sixteen orbital); estimated 10–30% backdrop shader saving. Low implementation risk, medium visual risk: mip popping/shimmer. Shader change at `scene.hlsl:866`.
3. **Cache decoded native tile layers and orbital noise.** Decode immutable tile indices once, retain live palette lookup; bake noise into a repeatable 3D texture. Estimated 20–60% affected shader saving; high correctness risk for scroll/scanline/VRAM changes. Shader/compute/upload changes at `scene.hlsl:605`, `:803`.
4. **Opaque cabin occlusion before sky.** Depth-only cabin prepass plus genuinely distant depth-tested backgrounds; estimate 5–20% eye saving depending on pose. High ordering/LRZ risk; current depth-zero mask path cannot provide this. Shader/pipeline changes may be needed (`application.cpp:1731`, `:1767`; `vulkan_scene_pipeline.cpp:38`).

Enhanced Sky replaces native upper geometry, not simply adding a duplicate sphere: full-sphere replacements clear native BG2; ordinary replacements retain lower ground and add photographs/bodies (`include/starfox/vr/enhanced_landscape.hpp:132`). OFF removes their filtering/blending but restores native tile decoding and sometimes expensive orbital noise. Thus OFF's net gain is scene-dependent: measure identical override 0/1 runs. Future shader edits require regeneration with `python3 tools/generate_vr_shaders.py --dxc ~/VulkanSDK/1.4.363.0/macOS/bin/dxc`.
